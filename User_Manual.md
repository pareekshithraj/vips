# Vidyabodhini User Manual 📚

Welcome to **Vidyabodhini**, your smart study companion! This guide will help you get started quickly.

---

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

### 1. Getting Started
*   **Sign Up**: Click "Get Started" on the home page.
*   **Profile Setup**: You'll be asked 4 quick questions:
    1.  **Class**: Select your current grade (8th - 12th).
    2.  **Syllabus**: Choose CBSE or State Board.
    3.  **Subjects**: Pick the subjects you are studying.
    4.  **Availability**: Tell us how many hours you can study each day.

### 2. Your Dashboard
Once set up, you will see your **Personalized Dashboard**:
*   **🔥 Streak (Top Header)**: Shows how many days in a row you've been active. Keep the flame burning!
*   **📅 Timetable (Main View)**: Your daily study plan.
    *   **Start**: Click "Start" on a scheduled chapter to begin studying.
    *   **Mark Done**: Click "Mark as Done" when finished to earn points.
*   **🏆 Leaderboard (Right Sidebar)**: See the top 10 students based on learning points.

### 3. Study Tools
*   **Library**: Browse all chapters and topics manually.
*   **Focus Mode**: A distraction-free timer for deep work.
*   **Profile**: Update your subjects or available hours anytime.

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Administrators

### 1. Accessing the Admin Portal
1.  Go to the **Landing Page** (Home).
2.  Scroll down to the footer (bottom of the page).
3.  Click on **Admin Portal**.
4.  Log in with your admin credentials.

### 2. Admin Features
*   **Dashboard Stats**: View real-time data:
    *   Total Registered Students
    *   Active Users Today
    *   Average Syllabus Completion
*   **Student Directory**: A searchable table of all students. You can filter by Class or Status to monitor engagement.

---

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What if I didn't study yesterday?**
A: Your streak will reset to 0. Try to log in every day!

**Q: Can I change my syllabus later?**
A: Yes, go to your **Profile** page to edit your academic details.

**Q: How do I earn points?**
A: You earn points by completing study tasks from your timetable.
